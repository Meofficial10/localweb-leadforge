# Design Brief

Two surfaces: the operator dashboard, and the auto-generated demo websites that carry the pitch.

## Dashboard
- Tone: calm, data-first, trustworthy (CRM-like).
- Left sidebar nav, top bar with campaign switcher + kill switch, content area with cards/tables.
- Light + dark themes, neutral grays, one accent (indigo/teal), status colors.
- Inter/system sans, 14px base, tabular numbers.
- LIVE mode toggle requires confirmation + persistent badge; kill switch always visible.
- WCAG AA contrast, keyboard nav, focus states.

## Demo website design system
- One page: hero (name + tagline), services, about, gallery (real/placeholder only), hours + map link, reviews highlight (paraphrased, never invented), contact buttons, footer.
- Theme packs by category: warm/soft (salon, spa), bold/clean (repair, trade), fresh/light (cafe, bakery), elegant (florist, boutique).
- Mobile-first, <100KB without images, accessible, semantic, no fake testimonials/prices/awards.
- Banner: "Demo preview by [sender]" with a contact link.

## Email style
- Plain text or light HTML; <120 words; first line references their business; one link; one opt-out line; no images/tracking-templates.

## Voice call style
- Friendly, brief, polite; discloses AI within first sentence; offers human callback; ends gracefully on hesitation.
